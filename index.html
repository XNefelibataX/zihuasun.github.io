Research experience

2026–Present

Real-Time Closed-Loop Motor-Imagery BCI for Rehabilitation Devices

Research Intern · BrainCo

  • Develop end-to-end motor-imagery BCI systems using dry- and wet-electrode EEG, from acquisition and quality control to online inference and hardware integration.
  • Build continuous left/right motor-intention decoding pipelines that control a pneumatic rehabilitation glove and robotic platforms.
  • Benchmark FBCSP+SVM and EEGNet, reaching approximately 84% single-session accuracy in representative high-quality dry-electrode recordings.
  • Study low-SNR EEG, cross-session shift, inter-subject variability, false activations, and lightweight online adaptation strategies.
2025–Present

M.S.E. Thesis: EEG Foundation Models for Robust Motor-Intention Decoding

University of Pennsylvania · Advisors: Prof. Michelle J. Johnson and Prof. Nadia Figueroa

  • Investigate REVE and LaBraM across healthy participants and individuals with stroke, emphasizing generalization, data efficiency, and domain shift.
  • Apply LoRA-based fine-tuning; REVE achieved approximately 95% mean left/right motor-imagery accuracy in the stroke cohort under subject-wise evaluation.
  • Conduct channel and temporal-window ablations and examine compact sensorimotor configurations, healthy-to-stroke transfer, and failure cases.
  • Extend the work toward pseudo-real-time and closed-loop AO/MI decoding with online adaptation and rehabilitation-robot feedback.
2025–Present

EEG-Based Motor Intention Decoding for Rehabilitation Robotics

Research Assistant · Rehabilitation Robotics Laboratory, University of Pennsylvania

  • Work with Prof. Michelle J. Johnson on EEG-based action observation and motor imagery for upper-limb rehabilitation and bilateral intention decoding.
  • Analyze motor-related mu/beta activity and spatial-temporal EEG representations across upper-limb actions under subject and session variability.
  • Integrate EEG decoding with rehabilitation robotics through real-time interfaces for closed-loop intention-to-robot feedback.
Summer 2025

Epidural Electrical Stimulation for Spinal Cord Injury Rehabilitation

Summer Researcher · Zhejiang University & Zhejiang University Second Affiliated Hospital

  • Analyzed multi-channel EMG responses under varying stimulation parameters and developed MATLAB/Python pipelines for artifact handling and quantitative analysis.
  • Extracted ΔARV and median-frequency features across stimulation channels, muscles, frequencies, and current levels.
  • Built an interactive Dash/Plotly system for exploring response maps, muscle recruitment, and stimulation-parameter effects.
2024–2025

Brain-Computer Interface for Robotic Fist Control

B.Eng. Honours Project · University of Edinburgh

  • Developed a four-class EEG decoder for real and imagined left/right fist movements using the PhysioNet EEG Motor Movement/Imagery dataset.
  • Enhanced EEGNet with 8–30 Hz motor-band filtering and dynamic channel attention, improving accuracy from 43.3% to approximately 60% with fewer than 9K trainable parameters.
  • Completed preprocessing, model development, ablations, five-fold cross-validation, comparisons, visualization, and performance analysis.
